## 1. Getting Started

### What is the Staff Portal?

The P.A.C.E. Staff Portal is your shop's internal management system. It replaces paper repair orders, spreadsheets, and manual tracking. From the portal you can:

- Manage appointment requests from the website and phone
- Create and track repair orders
- Run digital inspections
- Build estimates and send them to customers for approval
- Issue invoices and track payment status
- Keep a complete history for every customer and vehicle

The portal is for **staff use only**. Customers never see the portal — they only receive approval links and invoices by email.

---

### Logging In

1. Open a web browser and go to **powerautomotive.ca/portal**
2. Enter your staff username (`paceadmin`) or staff email address
3. Enter your 4-digit PIN
4. Click **Sign In**

If you enter the wrong PIN or username, you will see an error message. Try again or contact the shop owner to reset your PIN.

> **Tip:** Bookmark the portal address so you can find it quickly each day.

---

### Signing Out

Click the **Sign Out** button at the very bottom of the left sidebar.

Always sign out when you are done for the day, especially if using a shared computer.

---

### Inactivity Auto Sign-Out

If the portal is left open but unused for a period of time, it will automatically sign you out. This is a security feature to prevent unauthorized access.

You will see a warning message before sign-out happens. If you are still working, click the button in the warning to stay signed in.

---

### Navigating the Portal

The left side of the screen has a sidebar with links to every section:

| Sidebar Link | What It Does |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Overview of shop activity |
| Customers | Customer records |
| Vehicles | Vehicle records |
| Appointments | Appointment requests |
| Repair Orders | Active and past repair orders |
| Inspections | Vehicle inspection records |
| Estimates | Estimate builder |
| Invoices | Invoice list and Helcim links |
| **Presets** (bottom) | Reusable job templates (setup tool) |

The currently active page is highlighted in the sidebar. **Presets** appears at the bottom of the sidebar under a "Setup" label because it is a configuration tool, not a daily-use page.

The Dashboard is the first thing you see after logging in. It gives you a quick snapshot of what is happening in the shop right now.

---

## 3. Customers

The Customers page stores the contact information and service history for every person who has brought a vehicle to P.A.C.E.

---

### Viewing Customers

Click **Customers** in the sidebar. A list of all customers appears, sorted by most recently added.

---

### Searching Customers

Use the search bar at the top to find a customer by:

- First or last name
- Phone number
- Email address

Results update as you type.

---

### Adding a New Customer

1. Click the **New Customer** button (top right)
2. Fill in the form:
   - **First Name** and **Last Name** (required)
   - **Phone** (required — used for approvals and contact)
   - **Email** (used to send estimate approval links)
   - **Preferred Contact** — how the customer prefers to be reached
   - **Address** and **Notes** (optional)
3. Click **Save Customer**

> **Important:** Keep phone numbers and email addresses accurate. The portal sends estimate approval links to the customer's email on file. If the email is wrong, the customer will not receive them.

---

### Editing Customer Information

1. Click the customer's name in the list to open their record
2. Click **Edit**
3. Make your changes and click **Save**

---

### Customer Service History

When you open a customer record, you will see a **Service History** tab. This shows every repair order, inspection, estimate, and invoice linked to that customer in one place.

This is useful when a returning customer calls — you can quickly pull up everything that has ever been done on their vehicles.

## 5. Appointments

The Appointments page manages all appointment requests — whether they come in through the public website booking form, by phone, or as a walk-in.

---

### Viewing Appointments

Click **Appointments** in the sidebar. You will see a calendar view by default, and you can switch to a list view using the **Calendar / List** toggle in the top right.

**Calendar view:** Shows all appointment requests placed on the date they were submitted. Use the arrow buttons to navigate months. Click any appointment card to see its details below the calendar.

**List view:** Shows all appointments as a table. Use the search bar and status filter tabs to narrow results.

---

### Appointment Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | New request, not yet reviewed |
| Confirmed | Staff has confirmed the appointment with the customer |
| Cancelled | Appointment was cancelled |
| Converted | Appointment has been turned into a Repair Order |

---

### Adding a Manual Appointment Request

For phone calls or walk-ins that should be tracked:

1. Click **Add Request**
2. Select the **Source**: Phone or Walk-in
3. Fill in the customer's name, phone number, and what service they need
4. Enter a preferred date/time if known (free text — e.g., "Friday morning" or "any weekday after 2pm")
5. Click **Save Request**

> Web form requests from your website appear here automatically — you do not need to enter them manually.

---

### Converting an Appointment to a Repair Order

When a customer comes in or you are ready to create a file for their vehicle:

1. Click the appointment in the list or calendar
2. In the detail panel that appears, click **Convert to RO**
3. Select or create the **customer** (required)
4. Select or create the **vehicle** (required)
5. Enter mileage in, promised date, and any customer concerns
6. Click **Create Repair Order**

The appointment will be marked **Converted** and you will be taken to the new Repair Order.

## 6. Repair Orders

A Repair Order (RO) is the central record for everything done on a customer's vehicle during one shop visit. Inspections, estimates, and invoices are all linked to the RO.

---

### Creating a Repair Order

1. Click **Repair Orders** in the sidebar
2. Click **New Repair Order**
3. Select or create the **Customer**
4. Select or create the **Vehicle**
5. Add at least one **Customer Concern** — what the customer says is wrong (e.g., "Brakes grinding when stopping", "Check engine light on")
   - Click **+ Add Concern** to add more concerns if the customer has multiple issues
6. Fill in optional fields: Mileage In, Promised Date, Internal Notes (staff-only)
7. Click **Save Repair Order**

The RO will be assigned an RO number automatically (e.g., RO-2026-0031).

> **Tip:** Add each customer concern as a separate line. This makes it easy to build a structured estimate where each concern has its own section.

---

### Repair Order Statuses

| Status | When to Use |
|---|---|
| Draft | Just created, not yet started |
| Active | Vehicle is checked in, work is starting |
| In Progress | Technician is actively working |
| Waiting Approval | Estimate sent to customer, waiting for response |
| Approved | Customer approved — proceed with work |
| Completed | All work is done |
| Invoiced | Invoice has been issued |
| Closed | Fully paid and filed |
| Cancelled | Repair order was cancelled |

Change the status by clicking the status button in the RO detail panel.

---

### Viewing and Editing a Repair Order

Click any RO in the list to open its detail panel. From here you can:

- See all RO information
- Edit the RO (status, mileage, dates, notes)
- Print the Internal RO worksheet
- Open or create an Inspection
- Open or create an Estimate
- View linked invoices

## 7. Internal RO Printout

The Internal RO Printout is a paper worksheet designed for the shop floor. It gives the technician all the information they need to start working, with blank sections for hand-written notes.

---

### What It Is For

- Gives the mechanic the customer's name, vehicle info, and concerns
- Provides blank sections for diagnosis notes, parts/materials used, and work performed
- Acts as the paper record that travels with the vehicle during the repair

---

### How to Print It

1. Open the Repair Order
2. Click **Print Internal RO**
3. Your browser print dialog will open
4. Select your shop printer and click Print

> **Tip:** Disable "Headers and Footers" in your browser's print dialog for a cleaner printout without browser-generated text at the top and bottom.

---

### What Auto-Fills on the Printout

The following information fills in automatically from the RO:

- Customer name
- Vehicle year, make, model, trim, colour
- License plate and VIN
- Mileage in and out
- Promised date
- Numbered customer concerns

---

### Recommended Workflow with the Internal RO

1. **Create the RO** in the portal with customer concerns
2. **Print the Internal RO** worksheet
3. **Give the printout to the technician** with the vehicle keys
4. **Technician writes** their diagnosis, parts needed, and work performed on the paper
5. **Front desk staff updates** the estimate and inspection in the portal based on the technician's notes
6. **Send the estimate** to the customer for approval

> **Note:** Internal notes from the RO do not print on the customer-facing invoice. They are staff-only.

## 8. Inspections

Inspections let you document the condition of a customer's vehicle in a structured, professional way.

---

### What Inspections Are Used For

- Documenting vehicle condition at drop-off
- Flagging items for the customer's awareness (even if not being repaired today)
- Supporting estimate recommendations with documented findings
- Providing a record if questions arise later

---

### Starting an Inspection

1. Open the Repair Order for the vehicle
2. Click **Start Inspection** in the RO detail panel
3. The inspection opens in a structured format with categories (e.g., Brakes, Tires, Lights, Fluids)

---

### Adding Inspection Items

For each item you inspect:

1. Select the **Category**
2. Enter a **Description** (e.g., "Front brake pads")
3. Set the **Condition**: Good, Fair, Poor, or Critical
4. Add a **Note** if needed (e.g., "2mm remaining, recommend replacement")
5. The item saves automatically when you change its condition

---

### Inspection Photos

If photo uploads are available, you can attach a photo to any inspection item. This is useful for documenting damage or wear that the customer may want to see.

---

### Inspection and Estimate Relationship

Inspections and estimates are separate records, but they work together. After completing an inspection:

- Items flagged as Poor or Critical are typically added to the estimate as recommended repairs
- The inspection record stays on file for reference

## 9. Estimates

An estimate lists all the work and parts the shop recommends for a customer's vehicle, along with pricing. Customers review and approve estimates before work begins.

---

### Opening or Creating an Estimate

From the Repair Order detail panel, click **Create Estimate** or **Open Estimate**.

The estimate builder opens as a full-page view.

---

### Estimate Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Being built, not yet sent to customer |
| Sent | Approval link emailed to customer |
| Approved | Customer has approved (partially or fully) |
| Declined | Customer declined all items |
| Cancelled | Estimate was cancelled |

---

### Job Groups (Sections)

When an estimate is created from an RO that has multiple customer concerns, the portal automatically creates a **job group** (section) for each concern. For example:

> RO Concerns: "Brakes grinding" / "Oil change" / "Check engine light"
>
> → Estimate will have three sections: **Brakes Grinding**, **Oil Change**, **Check Engine Light**

Each section keeps related line items organized and makes the estimate easy for the customer to read and understand.

You can also add a job group manually by clicking **Add Job Group**, or remove an accidental group (the line items will move to the "Ungrouped" section — nothing is deleted).

---

### Adding Line Items

Click **Add Item** inside a job group (or in the Ungrouped section) to add a line item.

| Item Type | When to Use |
|---|---|
| Labour | Technician time (e.g., 1.5 hrs brake service) |
| Part | Parts and components (e.g., brake pads) |
| Shop Supply | Consumables and shop fees (e.g., brake cleaner) |
| Fee | Special fees (e.g., shop fee, hazmat disposal) |
| Discount | Apply a dollar or percentage discount |

For each line item, fill in:

- **Description** — what it is (e.g., "Front brake pad replacement — labour")
- **Quantity** — how many or how many hours
- **Customer Price** — what the customer will be charged per unit
- **Required** — if checked, the customer cannot decline this item
- **Customer Visible** — if unchecked, the item is hidden from the customer (internal use only)

See [Section 10](#10-pricing-and-markup) for information about internal cost and markup fields.

---

### Required vs Optional Items

- **Required items** are included in the estimate total regardless of what the customer chooses. The customer cannot decline these.
- **Optional items** can be individually approved or declined by the customer.

Use required items for work that must be done (e.g., labour for diagnosing a safety issue). Use optional items for additional recommendations the customer can choose to accept or decline.

---

### Customer Message

There is a **Customer Message** field at the top of the estimate builder. This is a personal note to the customer that will appear at the top of their approval page (e.g., "Hi John, here is the estimate for your Camry. Please review and approve the items below.").

---

### Totals Bar

At the bottom of the estimate builder, a totals bar shows:

- **Subtotal** — all customer-visible line items combined
- **GST (5%)** — calculated automatically
- **Total** — subtotal + GST
- **Approved Total** — the total of items the customer has approved (if approval has been sent)

---

### Sending the Estimate for Approval

1. Scroll to the **Customer Actions** section at the bottom of the estimate
2. Confirm the customer's email address
3. Click **Send Approval Link**
4. The customer will receive an email with a secure link to review and approve their estimate

After sending, the estimate status changes to **Sent**.

---

### Customer Response

Once the customer approves or declines items, the estimate updates automatically. You can see which items were approved, which were declined, and what the approved total is.

---

## 10. Pricing and Markup

The portal supports internal cost and markup tracking. This is for staff only — customers never see cost or markup information.

---

### The Three Pricing Fields

When adding a line item to an estimate or preset, you will see:

| Field | Who Sees It | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Internal Cost | Staff only | What the shop pays for the part or labour |
| Markup | Staff only | How much to add on top of cost |
| Customer Price | Customer sees this | The final price shown on the estimate and approval |

---

### How Markup Works

You can choose one of two markup methods:

**Percent Markup**
> Cost $50.00 + 25% markup = **Customer Price $62.50**

**Fixed Dollar Markup**
> Cost $50.00 + $20.00 fixed = **Customer Price $70.00**

When you enter the cost and markup, the customer price is calculated for you automatically. You can also override the customer price directly if needed.

---

### Safety: Cost and Markup Are Never Shown to Customers

- Internal cost and markup **do not appear** on the customer approval page
- They **do not appear** on the customer invoice
- They **do not appear** in the email sent to the customer
- They **do not appear** on the Helcim payment invoice

> **You do not need to worry about accidentally exposing cost information.** The portal is designed to keep this information internal at all times.

---

## 11. Presets

Presets are reusable job templates that let you quickly add common repairs to any estimate without entering the same information every time.

---

### What Is a Preset?

A **Preset** is a saved collection of line items for a common job. For example:

- **Oil Change** — 1 labour hour + oil filter part + shop supplies
- **Brake Service (Front)** — labour + brake pads + brake cleaner + shop fee
- **Diagnostic** — 1 diagnostic labour hour
- **Alignment** — 1 alignment labour hour

Instead of typing these items every time, you can apply the entire preset to an estimate with one click.

---

### Presets vs Job Groups

These two things are different:

| | Preset | Job Group |
|---|---|---|
| What is it? | Saved template you can reuse | A section inside one specific estimate |
| Where is it? | Presets page (sidebar bottom) | Inside an estimate |
| Who creates it? | Admin/owner, done once | Created from RO concerns or manually |
| When used? | Applied to any estimate | Organizes work in one estimate |

---

### Opening the Presets Page

Click **Presets** in the bottom of the sidebar (under the "Setup" label).

---

### Creating a Preset

1. Click **New Preset**
2. Enter a **Name** (e.g., "Front Brake Service")
3. Enter a **Category** (e.g., "Brakes") — used to organize presets in the picker
4. Enter a **Description** if helpful (e.g., "Labour + pads for one axle")
5. Click **Save**
6. In the right panel, click **Add Item** to add line items to this preset
   - Enter description, quantity, internal cost (optional), markup (optional), and customer price
7. Add as many items as needed

---

### Editing a Preset

1. Click the preset name in the left panel
2. Edit the name, description, or category in the right panel and click **Save**
3. Edit individual items using the pencil icon next to each item

---

### Deactivating a Preset

If a preset is no longer needed, click **Deactivate** rather than deleting it. Deactivated presets:

- No longer appear in the "Add Preset Job" picker in the estimate builder
- Still appear on the Presets page with an "Inactive" badge
- Can be **reactivated** at any time

> Presets are never permanently deleted — only deactivated.

---

### Using a Preset in an Estimate

1. Open an estimate
2. Click **Add Preset Job** (in the estimate builder toolbar)
3. The preset picker opens, organized by category
4. Preview the line items in the preset
5. Click **Add to Estimate**
6. All the preset's line items are added to the estimate in a new job group

You can then edit, add, or remove individual items within the estimate without affecting the original preset.

## 12. Customer Approval Workflow

The approval workflow lets customers review and approve their estimate online, without needing to be in the shop.

---

### How It Works — Step by Step

1. **Staff builds the estimate** in the portal
2. **Staff sends the approval link** — the customer receives an email with a secure link
3. **Customer opens the link** on their phone or computer (no login required)
4. **Customer reviews the estimate** — they see a clean list of items with descriptions and prices
5. **Customer approves or declines** optional items. Required items cannot be declined.
6. **Customer submits their response**
7. **The portal updates** — the estimate shows approved and declined items
8. **Staff receives the update** and can proceed with work on approved items
9. **Staff creates the invoice** from the approved estimate

---

### Important Notes About Approval Links

- The link is single-use — once the customer submits, it cannot be resubmitted
- The link expires after 7 days
- The customer does **not** need to create an account or log in
- The customer sees prices but **never sees** internal cost, markup, or staff-only notes

---

### After Approval

Once you see that the customer has approved:

1. Check the estimate for approved items
2. Proceed with work
3. When complete, go to the Repair Order and create or link an invoice

## 14. Customer Invoice Printout

The Customer Invoice Printout is a professional, formatted invoice you can print for the customer or save as a PDF.

---

### How to Print

1. Open the Invoice in the portal
2. Click **Print Invoice**
3. Your browser print dialog will open
4. Choose your printer, or select **Save as PDF** to create a digital copy

> **For a clean printout:** In the print dialog, turn off "Headers and Footers" (sometimes called "Print headers and footers"). This prevents the browser from adding the web address and date at the top and bottom of the page.

---

### Suggested File Names (for PDF saves)

| Format | Example |
|---|---|
| By invoice number | PACE_INV001012 |
| By RO number | PACE_RO-2026-0029 |

---

### What Appears on the Printout

- Shop name and contact information (P.A.C.E. / Power Automotive Centre of Excellence)
- Invoice number and date
- Customer name and contact
- Vehicle details (year, make, model, trim, colour, VIN, plate)
- Customer concerns
- Line items (description, quantity, unit price, total)
- Subtotal, GST (5%), and total
- Amount paid and balance due
- Notes (customer-facing only)
- Signature block and legal text

---

### What Does NOT Appear on the Printout

The following are **never printed** on the customer invoice:

- Internal cost (what the shop pays)
- Markup percentage or dollar amount
- Staff-only internal notes
- Approval tokens or security codes
- Any card or payment data

> Customers only see their final prices. Internal shop information stays internal.

## 15. Recommended Daily Workflow

This is a typical start-to-finish flow for a customer visit. Not every step applies to every situation — use what fits.

---

### Morning (Start of Day)

1. **Log in** to the portal
2. **Check the Dashboard** for overnight web bookings and pending approval responses
3. **Review Appointments** — confirm any incoming vehicles for the day
4. **Update RO statuses** for vehicles coming in today (Draft → Active)

---

### When a Customer Arrives

5. **Convert appointment to RO** (if not already done), or **create a new RO** directly
6. **Confirm customer and vehicle info** — update if anything has changed
7. **Add customer concerns** to the RO — use their exact words
8. **Print the Internal RO** worksheet and hand it to the technician with the keys

---

### During the Repair

9. **Technician diagnoses** and writes findings on the paper worksheet
10. **Staff enters inspection items** in the Inspections page based on technician findings
11. **Staff builds the estimate** using the estimate builder:
    - Add preset jobs for common work
    - Add manual line items for parts and labour
    - Enter internal costs and markup if used
    - Set required vs optional items
12. **Send the estimate** to the customer for approval

---

### After Approval

13. **Customer approves** the estimate (via email link)
14. **Check approved items** in the portal
15. **Technician completes** the approved work
16. **Update RO status** to Completed
17. **Create or link the Helcim invoice**
18. **Take payment** via Helcim terminal
19. **Confirm payment status** updated in the portal
20. **Print the customer invoice** if needed
21. **Update RO status** to Closed

---

## 16. Common Scenarios

### Customer Calls to Book an Appointment

1. Click **Appointments** → **Add Request**
2. Set Source to **Phone**
3. Enter name, phone, service needed, and preferred time
4. Click **Save Request**
5. Call the customer back to confirm a time, then update the status to **Confirmed**

---

### Walk-In Customer

1. Click **Appointments** → **Add Request**
2. Set Source to **Walk-in**
3. Fill in details and save
4. Immediately convert to an RO: click the appointment → **Convert to RO**

---

### Existing Customer Returns

1. Search for the customer by name or phone in the **Customers** page
2. Click their name to see their history
3. Create a new RO directly from the RO page (select their existing customer and vehicle records)

---

### Customer Has Multiple Concerns

1. When creating the RO, click **+ Add Concern** for each issue
2. When the estimate is created, each concern automatically becomes its own section
3. Add the appropriate line items under each section

---

### Mechanic Finds Extra Work

1. Contact the customer before doing any additional work
2. Open the estimate and add the new items
3. Re-send the approval link (or confirm by phone and note it in internal notes)
4. Proceed once the customer has approved

---

### Customer Declines Part of the Estimate

1. After the approval link is submitted, check the estimate
2. Items the customer declined will show as **Declined**
3. Only proceed with work the customer approved
4. The invoice will reflect only approved items

---

### Invoice Is Paid In Person

1. Process the payment through the Helcim terminal as normal
2. The portal will automatically update the payment status within a short time
3. If the status has not updated, use the Sync button in the invoice detail panel
4. Update the RO status to **Closed** once everything is settled

---

### Staff Accidentally Creates the Wrong Job Group in an Estimate

1. Open the estimate
2. Click the **remove** button on the unwanted job group
3. Confirm the prompt: "Remove this job group? Line items will be moved to Ungrouped."
4. The job group disappears and its line items move to the Ungrouped section — nothing is deleted
5. Drag or reassign items to the correct section as needed

---

### Staff Needs to Create a New Preset

1. Click **Presets** at the bottom of the sidebar
2. Click **New Preset**
3. Enter the name and category
4. Add line items with descriptions, quantities, and pricing
5. Save — the preset is now available in the estimate builder

---

| Document | How to Print |
|---|---|
| Internal RO worksheet | Open RO → Print Internal RO |
| Customer invoice | Open Invoice → Print Invoice |

In both cases, disable browser headers/footers in the print dialog for a clean result.

## 17. What Not To Do

These are important rules to keep the portal running cleanly and securely.

---

| Do Not | Why |
|---|---|
| Share the staff login | If someone leaves, there is no way to know who made changes. Protect the password. |
| Put internal cost or markup in customer-facing notes or descriptions | Customers see those fields on their approval page. Keep cost information in the designated markup fields only. |
| Use fake names or test data during real operation | Test records mix with real customer data and are hard to clean up. |
| Delete records when something goes wrong | Use Cancelled, Closed, Archived, Deactivated, or Inactive instead. Records can always be reviewed later. |
| Share customer approval links publicly | Approval links are for the specific customer only. Do not post them publicly or forward to the wrong person. |
| Edit a Helcim invoice manually without reason | Helcim invoices are the financial record. Changes should match what is in the portal. |
| Write card numbers or CVV codes in notes | Card data should never be stored anywhere in the portal. Helcim handles all payment data securely. |

## 18. Troubleshooting

### Approval email did not send

- Confirm the customer's email address is correct in the Customers page
- Ask the customer to check their spam or junk folder
- Try sending the approval link again from the estimate builder
- If the problem persists, you can share the direct link by copying it from the sent estimate

---

### Invoice did not sync with Helcim

- Check that the invoice has a linked Helcim Invoice ID
- Use the Sync button in the invoice detail panel
- If the payment was recent, wait a few minutes and check again — sync can take a moment
- Contact the portal administrator if the problem persists

---

### Customer cannot open the approval link

- The link may have expired (links expire after 7 days). Send a new approval link.
- The link may have already been submitted. Check the estimate status — if it shows Approved or Declined, the customer already responded.
- Make sure the customer is opening the link in a browser (not just previewing in email)

---

### Printout looks wrong (extra lines, URL at top, date at bottom)

- In your browser's print dialog, find the option for **Headers and Footers** and turn it off
- This setting is usually under "More settings" or "Additional settings" depending on your browser

---

### Browser adds the date or URL to the printout

Same as above — turn off Headers and Footers in the browser print dialog.

---

### Portal signs out automatically

- This is the inactivity auto sign-out feature. It is normal and is a security measure.
- When you see the warning, click the button to stay signed in
- If you are doing a longer task (like building an estimate), keep the tab active

---

### A page does not load or shows an error

1. Refresh the page (press F5 or click the browser refresh button)
2. If the error continues, sign out and sign back in
3. If the problem persists, contact the portal administrator

---

### Customer or vehicle is missing from a selector

- The customer or vehicle may not have been created yet. Go to Customers or Vehicles and create the record first.
- Use the search bar in the selector to confirm they are not there with a slightly different spelling

---

### Totals look wrong on the estimate

- Check that all line item quantities and unit prices are correct
- Confirm that no items are accidentally hidden (customer visibility)
- GST (5%) is calculated automatically on the subtotal
- If a discount item was added, confirm it has the correct amount

---

### A preset does not appear in the picker

- The preset may have been deactivated. Go to the Presets page (sidebar bottom), find the preset, and click **Reactivate**.
- Confirm the preset has at least one active line item
